Her Health Collective Website – Our Kits Page

Update the Our Kits page while maintaining the existing visual identity of the website.

Keep the current colour palette, typography, navigation, rounded corners, card style, and minimalist nonprofit aesthetic.

Do not redesign the website from scratch.

The page should feel warm, practical, trustworthy, and community-focused.

Use generous white space and avoid overcrowding the page.

Do not rewrite approved content unless specifically instructed.

────────────────────────

GLOBAL WEBSITE FIXES

Apply these fixes across the entire website:

• Every navigation link should open the destination page at the top.

• Every card, button, or call-to-action that opens another page should load that page at the top.

• Preserve scroll position only when the user returns with the browser Back button.

• Remove all nested buttons and nested links.

• Never place a button inside another button.

• Never place a clickable card around a separate button.

• Use valid semantic HTML for every interactive element.

• Ensure all links and buttons are keyboard accessible.

• Add visible focus states.

────────────────────────

PAGE HEADER

Keep the page header simple and welcoming.

Include:

Title:
Our Kits

Supporting text:
Practical care kits created to help reduce barriers to health, hygiene, and well-being in our community.

Do not use a large hero image.

A small product photograph or simple kit illustration may be used if it already exists, but the page should remain minimal.

────────────────────────

INTRODUCTION

Add a short introductory section explaining:

• What the kits are
• Who they are intended to support
• Why access to basic health and hygiene products matters
• That kits are distributed through community partnerships and outreach

Keep the wording concise.

Do not make claims about current distribution numbers, impact, partnerships, or availability unless that information already exists on the website.

KIT TYPES

Create a clean section titled:

What’s Inside

Display the available kit types using simple cards.

Possible kit types may include:

• Menstrual Care Kits
• Personal Hygiene Kits
• Sexual Health Kits
• Comfort and Wellness Kits

Only include kit types that Her Health actually plans to offer.

Do not invent products, programs, or partnerships.

Each kit card should contain:

• Kit name
• One-sentence purpose
• A short list of typical contents
• A simple icon or small image
• Optional availability badge

Possible availability badges:

Available
Coming Soon
In Development

Do not place an interactive button inside a card that is already clickable.

────────────────────────

KIT CONTENTS

For each kit, present contents in a clean, easy-to-scan list.

Examples of layout only:

• Product name
• Quantity
• Short explanation when needed

Avoid making the page look like an online store.

Do not include pricing, shopping carts, checkout buttons, or commercial product language.

The kits are community-support resources, not retail products.

────────────────────────

Add a section titled:

How It Works

ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

• Strong colour contrast
• Large readable text
• Clear heading hierarchy
• Keyboard-accessible controls
• Visible focus indicators
• Descriptive button labels
• Alt text support for images
• Mobile-friendly layouts
• No nested buttons
• No nested links
• No inaccessible clickable card structures

────────────────────────

WRITING STYLE

Use:

• Canadian English
• Title Case headings
• Oxford comma
• No em dashes
• Warm, respectful, and practical language
• Grade 8–9 reading level
• First-person voice when referring to Her Health

Do not rewrite approved copy unless specifically instructed.

────────────────────────

FINAL GOAL

The Our Kits page should clearly explain what the kits are, why they matter, what they may include, and how the program works.

It should feel like a transparent and thoughtful community initiative, not an online shop.

The page should be minimal, welcoming, accessible, and consistent with the rest of the Her Health Collective website.
