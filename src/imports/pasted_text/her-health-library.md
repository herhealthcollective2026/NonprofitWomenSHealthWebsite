Her Health Collective Website – Her Health Library Page

Update the Her Health Library page while maintaining the existing visual identity of the website.

Keep the current colour palette, typography, rounded corners, card style, navigation, and overall warm nonprofit aesthetic.

Do not redesign the website from scratch.

Use a minimal, clean layout with generous white space. Avoid adding unnecessary sections, excessive icons, large amounts of decorative content, or too many cards at once.

IMPORTANT CODE FIX

Audit the existing website code and fix any invalid nested interactive elements.

Never place a <button> inside another <button>.

Never place a clickable button inside a card that is already implemented as a button.

For clickable cards, use one accessible outer link or card container and keep any inner labels non-interactive.

Ensure all interactive elements use valid semantic HTML and can be accessed by keyboard.

Do not change the approved written content unless necessary to correct a clear spelling or grammar error.

FEATURED ARTICLES

Start Here

Display no more than three featured article cards.

Use introductory articles that would be helpful to a first-time visitor.

Each card should include:

• Category label
• Article title
• Short description of no more than two sentences
• Button labelled “Read Article”

Keep the cards visually simple and consistent.

Do not use large photos on every card.

A small icon or subtle visual accent may be used, but it is not required.

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER

Include a concise medical disclaimer near the bottom of the page or in the footer.

Use this wording:

The information in the Her Health Library is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Keep the disclaimer readable but visually subtle.

────────────────────────

REMOVE UNNECESSARY CONTENT

Do not add:

• Advocacy previews
• “Coming Soon” advocacy sections
• Large statistics sections
• Testimonials
• Donation requests
• Newsletter pop-ups
• Multiple calls to action that repeat the same purpose
• Excessive category cards
• Decorative illustrations that make the page feel crowded

The page should focus only on helping visitors find and read reliable health information.

────────────────────────

ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

• Strong colour contrast
• Large, readable body text
• Clear heading hierarchy
• Keyboard-accessible controls
• Visible focus states
• Descriptive link and button labels
• Comfortable spacing between interactive elements
• Responsive mobile layout
• No nested buttons
• No nested links
• No card implemented as a button when it also contains another button

────────────────────────

WRITING STYLE

Use:

• Canadian English
• Title Case for headings
• Oxford comma
• No em dashes
• Warm and empowering language
• Plain language at approximately a Grade 8–9 reading level

Do not rewrite approved article titles, descriptions, or organizational messaging unless specifically instructed.

────────────────────────

FINAL GOAL

The Her Health Library page should feel calm, trustworthy, minimal, and easy to scan.

Visitors should be able to search, select a category, understand what each article covers, and begin reading without feeling overwhelmed.

Prioritize clarity, accessibility, and reliable health education over decorative design.
