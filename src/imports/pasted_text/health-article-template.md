Her Health Collective Website – Individual Health Article Template

Create the template used for every article in the Her Health Library.

Maintain the existing visual identity of the website.

Keep the current colours, typography, spacing, rounded corners, navigation, and minimalist nonprofit aesthetic.

This template should become the standard layout used for every health article published by Her Health Collective.

Do not redesign the website.

Refine the layout so it feels trustworthy, calm, professional, and easy to read.

Do not change any approved written content unless specifically instructed.

────────────────────────

GLOBAL WEBSITE IMPROVEMENTS

Apply these improvements across the entire website.

• Every page navigation should automatically scroll to the top of the page when opened.

• Every button that opens another page should automatically load that page at the top.

• Preserve the user's scroll position only when returning using the browser Back button.

• Remove any nested buttons or nested links.

• Ensure all interactive elements follow proper semantic HTML.

• Improve keyboard accessibility.

────────────────────────

PAGE HEADER

At the top of every article include:

Category

Article Title

One sentence summary

Reading Time

Last Reviewed date

Do not include a large hero image.

Keep the top section clean and uncluttered.

────────────────────────

TABLE OF CONTENTS

Immediately below the introduction include a small sticky table of contents.

Example:

In This Article

• Overview

• Symptoms

• Causes

• Risk Factors

• Diagnosis

• Treatment

• Prevention

• When to Seek Medical Care

• Frequently Asked Questions

• Trusted Sources

The table of contents should smoothly scroll to each section.

────────────────────────

ARTICLE CONTENT

Use generous spacing.

Limit text width for readability.

Use clear headings.

Break information into short paragraphs.

Use bullet lists where appropriate.

Avoid long walls of text.

Include optional information boxes for:

Quick Tip

Did You Know?

Important

These should be visually subtle.

────────────────────────

STANDARD ARTICLE STRUCTURE

Every article should follow this order.

Overview

Symptoms

Causes

Risk Factors

Diagnosis

Treatment

Prevention

When to Seek Medical Care

Frequently Asked Questions

Trusted Sources

References

Related Articles

Medical Disclaimer

Do not remove sections unless they truly do not apply.

────────────────────────

QUICK FACTS

Near the top include a simple Quick Facts card.

Possible fields:

Common Age

Common Symptoms

Is it Preventable?

When to See a Doctor

Do not make this visually overwhelming.

────────────────────────

RELATED ARTICLES

Near the bottom include:

Related Articles

Display three cards.

Use the same article card style used throughout the website.

Buttons should read:

Read Article

────────────────────────

TRUSTED SOURCES

Create a dedicated section called:

Trusted Sources

This should display trusted organizations referenced in the article.

Examples:

Government of Canada

Ontario Health

Women's College Hospital

SOGC

Cancer Care Ontario

Only display organizations actually used for that article.

────────────────────────

REFERENCES

Create a clean references section.

Keep citations easy to scan.

Do not overwhelm users with academic formatting.

────────────────────────

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER

Include the standard disclaimer at the bottom.

The information provided by Her Health Collective is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

────────────────────────

SHARE SECTION

At the bottom include:

Share this article

Copy Link

Email

LinkedIn

Do not include excessive social media sharing buttons.

────────────────────────

READABILITY

Maximum reading width approximately 700–800 pixels.

Comfortable line spacing.

Clear heading hierarchy.

Large readable fonts.

Consistent spacing between sections.

Avoid visual clutter.

────────────────────────

ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure:

• High colour contrast

• Keyboard accessibility

• Screen-reader friendly headings

• Proper heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)

• Alt text support for images

• Focus indicators

• No nested buttons

• No nested links

• Proper semantic HTML

────────────────────────

WRITING STYLE

Use:

Canadian English

Title Case headings

Oxford comma

No em dashes

Warm, supportive language

Grade 8–9 reading level

Preserve approved article wording whenever possible.

────────────────────────

DO NOT ADD

Testimonials

Donation requests

Advocacy sections

Newsletter pop-ups

Large hero banners

Auto-playing media

Random decorative illustrations

────────────────────────

FINAL GOAL

The article template should feel like reading information from a trusted Canadian health organization.

It should be calm, evidence-based, highly readable, accessible, and distraction-free.

The content should always remain the focus.
